Johnson's Lasagna

A literal nightmare block of cheese.

Ingredients

  • Blue cheese
  • Cheddar cheese
  • Fromunda cheese
  • Nacho cheese

steps

  1. Gather cheese.
  2. Chop, melt, spread cheese into a 3x6 baking pan.
  3. Place cheese in a humidifier for 13.4 hours.
  4. Serve to friends and family!
