Johnson's Lasagna

Behold, juicy pasta.

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Cheese
  • Elbow grease
  • Unfiltered crude oil. The venezuelan stuff is best

steps

  1. Fill your grill with charcoal.
  2. Carefully ball your ingredients. Make sure the elbow grease is coating the entirety of the ball.
  3. Grill for 16 hours, spraying it every hour with almond milk
  4. Serve to friends and family!
